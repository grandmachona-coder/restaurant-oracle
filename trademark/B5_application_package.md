## Risk Acknowledgement (Path A)

Filing without professional clearance accepts these risks:
1. **§2(d) refusal** — examiner finds prior similar mark I missed. Cost: $700 abandoned + new filing.
2. **§2(e)(1) descriptiveness** — examiner argues "Bistro Steward" merely describes software for restaurant management. B3 brief rated this LOW risk (suggestive compound), but examiner discretion varies.
3. **Opposition** — third party with prior rights opposes during 30-day window. TTAB proceeding ~$5-15k to defend.

If any of these land, retainer cost for response: ~$1,500-3,000 per office action.

## 3. Where Bistro Steward wins
- **Cheapest full-feature suite.** BS is the only product with recipe costing + **AI photo scan + barcode** + prep + ordering + POS + multi-location at **$29–99 flat**. Everything cheaper (BlueCart $10, Meez $19) is feature-thin. Comparable *full* suites: MarketMan $199+/loc, MarginEdge $350/loc, R365 $469+/loc, Crunchtime $5k+ — **2× to 50× more**.
- **$0 setup, free to start.** Free 30-day trial, no card, no setup fee — on a 6-month commitment up front (vs MarketMan +$1,500 setup + 12-mo lock; R365/MarginEdge annual commitments; Crunchtime $13k–90k first year). Half the lock-in of MarketMan, a fraction of the cost.
- **Flat vs per-location.** Multi-unit operators get crushed on per-location pricing elsewhere; BS Scale is flat $99.
- **AI photo inventory scan at SMB price.** Only enterprise-tier players (Apicbase, Crunchtime) match it; the cheap recipe tools (Meez, Galley, Parsley) don't have it at all.
- **Receipt OCR cost feed — now shipped.** Scanned invoices/receipts auto-update ingredient/size costs so recipes re-cost themselves — closing the prior #1 gap and reaching parity with the AP-automation incumbents (MarketMan, MarginEdge, R365, Craftable). BS is now ✅ on **every** capability row.

## 4. Where Bistro Steward lags
- **Done-for-you / depth at the top end:** MarginEdge's human invoice processing (handles handwriting, 24–48h), R365's full restaurant GL accounting, Crunchtime's labor+scheduling+AvT — BS is BOH-only, no labor/accounting suite.
- **POS breadth:** R365 70+, MarginEdge 50+, MarketMan 40+ POS. BS = Toast.
- **Track record / scale proof:** incumbents have years of reviews + enterprise references; BS is new.

## 5. Positioning takeaway
**BS is priced *well under market* for its capability tier.** The only cheaper things are a procurement app (BlueCart $10 + commission) and a recipe-only tool (Meez $19). Every product that matches BS's *breadth* (full inventory + costing + ordering + AI scan + OCR) starts at **$199–$350/location** or is contact-sales enterprise.

**Closest direct competitors:** (1) **MarketMan** — same SMB BOH job, but ~4–8× the price + per-location + $1,500 setup; (2) **Parsley** — kitchen-management at $129+, no AI/photo scan, OCR is a paid add-on; (3) **Meez Scale ($179)** — closest on the recipe/cost layer, but weak inventory, no barcode, POS in beta.

**One-line:** Bistro Steward's wedge = *full enterprise-grade capability (AI scan + receipt OCR + complete BOH) at a flat SMB price with $0 setup, undercutting the $200–500/location incumbents by 2–5×.* With OCR shipped, BS is now at feature parity — and wins on price.

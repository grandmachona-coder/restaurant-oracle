# Bistro Steward is Live

We're excited to announce that **Restaurant Oracle is now Bistro Steward** — same platform, same team, sharper name, and a new home at **bistrosteward.com**.

## Why the new name?

When we started, "Restaurant Oracle" captured the spirit of what we wanted to build: an always-on advisor sitting next to chefs and operators, surfacing the answer before they had to go looking. The product still does exactly that. The name, however, ran into a wall — Oracle Corporation's trademark footprint is broad, and operating under a name that even rhymes with theirs creates real risk for our customers and for us.

So we picked something better. **Bistro Steward** says what the product actually is: a steward for your restaurant. It watches inventory, costs out every recipe, flags margin drift, and quietly does the bookkeeping work that nobody on the line has time for. It's the role we've been playing all along; we just needed a name that fit.

## What's changing for you

- **Domain:** bistrosteward.com (the old URL redirects)
- **Email:** all support and notification mail now comes from `@bistrosteward.com`
- **App icon and brand:** refreshed, but the UI you know is intact
- **Your data:** untouched. Same Firebase backend, same login, same recipes, same inventory, same scans.

## What's not changing

The team. The roadmap. The pricing. The promise that every dollar of food cost passes through the same audit trail your accountant would build, only faster.

If you have the old domain bookmarked, swap it for **bistrosteward.com** at your convenience. If you spot any stale "Restaurant Oracle" reference still floating around — an email signature, a doc link, a footer — let us know and we'll squash it.

Thanks for building this with us. The next chapter is going to be a good one.

— The Bistro Steward team

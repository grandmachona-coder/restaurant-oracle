The Bistro Steward Loop

Your whole back-of-house, in one closed loop

Bistro Steward connects the six things every kitchen juggles — menu, recipes, prep, inventory, shopping, and vendors — into a single living loop. Change one, and the others update themselves. No more spreadsheets that drift apart by Friday.

1
📋
Menu
Define every dish you serve and the standing prep targets — how many ramekins of hollandaise Saturday brunch needs, how many empanadas to roll for Friday service.
2
📖
Recipes
Each menu item links to a recipe with an ingredients list and yield. Scale it, sub an ingredient, update a garnish — the whole loop reflows automatically.
3
🔪
Prep
Targets + recipes become a daily prep list — how many batches of each sub-recipe to fire, sorted by what's furthest below target. Your line cook starts their shift with a checklist, not a guessing game.
4
📦
Inventory
Print a count sheet by area — walk-in, dry storage, bar, prep table — and jot quantities as you walk. Snap a photo of the filled-in sheet and AI photo-scan reads your handwriting and auto-imports every count.
5
🛒
Shopping List
Oracle compares what your menu will need against what you already have and auto-populates a shopping list with exactly what to buy — quantity, unit, and which menu item drove each line.
6
🏢
Vendors
Each ingredient is tagged to the vendor you buy it from. The shopping list auto-groups by vendor, and one click emails them the order — line items, quantities, units — straight to your contact.
